About

I am a Mechanical Engineer and Research Engineer, currently working at CERTH on research and development projects involving energy systems and scientific software.

My research interests lie primarily in control engineering and artificial intelligence, with a particular interest in model predictive control, system identification, physics-informed machine learning, optimization, and embedded/edge AI. I am especially interested in approaches that combine physical models with data-driven methods and in their application to real-world dynamical systems.

My work has spanned control and optimization, machine learning, signal processing, embedded systems, scientific software development, and energy applications. I particularly enjoy problems that sit at the intersection of mechanical, electrical, and software engineering, connecting mathematical modelling and algorithms with physical systems and experimental data.

About

I am a Mechanical Engineer and Research Engineer, currently working at CERTH on research and development projects involving energy systems and scientific software.

My research interests lie primarily in control engineering and artificial intelligence, with a particular interest in model predictive control, system identification, physics-informed machine learning, optimization, and embedded/edge AI. I am especially interested in approaches that combine physical models with data-driven methods and in their application to real-world dynamical systems.

My work has spanned control and optimization, machine learning, signal processing, embedded systems, scientific software development, and energy applications. I particularly enjoy problems that sit at the intersection of mechanical, electrical, and software engineering, connecting mathematical modelling and algorithms with physical systems and experimental data.

Open-Source Development for Home Assistant

SONOFF SWV · Zigbee / ZHA

Contributing to the Home Assistant open-source ecosystem through device integration development. Current work adds timed watering support for the SONOFF SWV Zigbee irrigation valve, using device-side timing to allow autonomous valve operation without requiring Home Assistant to remain available.

The contribution includes new Home Assistant entities, Zigbee command handling, testing with physical hardware, automated tests and project quality checks.

Research Project · Ongoing

Thermal Modelling & System Identification

Residential Thermal Digital Twin

Development of a residential thermal digital twin using real indoor and outdoor measurements collected through Home Assistant. The project combines physical modelling, system identification and data-driven methods to model indoor temperature dynamics and evaluate thermal behaviour.

Current work explores first-order thermal models, parameter estimation, multi-step prediction and physics-informed machine learning, with the longer-term objective of integrating the models into model-based control and MPC.
